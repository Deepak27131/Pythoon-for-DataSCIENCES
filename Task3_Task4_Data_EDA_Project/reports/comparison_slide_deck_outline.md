# Bonus: 5-Slide Comparison Deck Outline

## Slide 1: Project Overview

- Telecom churn project: business/customer retention data
- Netflix project: entertainment/content catalog data
- Goal: compare how EDA supports decisions in different domains

## Slide 2: Data Cleaning Comparison

- Churn data needed numeric conversion for `TotalCharges`
- Netflix data needed date parsing and missing text cleanup
- Both projects required careful handling of missing or inconsistent values

## Slide 3: Feature Engineering Comparison

- Churn: `TenureGroup`, `AvgMonthlySpend`, encoded service/contract fields
- Netflix: `release_decade`, `is_movie`, country and genre extraction

## Slide 4: Key Insights

- Churn insights support retention, pricing, and contract strategy
- Netflix insights support catalog planning, country focus, and genre strategy

## Slide 5: What Students Learned

- Cleaning real-world datasets
- Creating useful features
- Visualizing patterns with Seaborn
- Turning charts into business or content recommendations
